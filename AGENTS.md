# UMTO mission authoring

For every future UMTO and Little Panth mission, plan a purposeful shot list before implementing the story. Use the shared cinematic camera described in [missions/MISSION_FORMAT.md](missions/MISSION_FORMAT.md). Establish locations with wides, frame dialogue with medium or two-shots, use reaction/insert close-ups for important beats, track meaningful movement, and use restrained pan/dolly/parallax when the scene benefits. High/low/overhead/POV/OTS require accurate viewpoint art or a genuine 3D scene; never distort flat sprites to fake perspective. Use Dutch angle only for tension. Keep interactive targets visible in play mode and keep captions separate from camera transforms. Honor reduced motion, keep spiritual/educational moments calm, and ensure the same shot list works in watch mode and export. Test camera math, story flows, and mobile/browser presentation before claiming deployment is verified.
