LITTLE PANTH · A PUNJABI VILLAGE ADVENTURE

The Cart That
Was Never Full

Help Chacha Baldev harvest wheat, load his bullock cart, and learn why having more isn't always happiness.

Five acts · cut • collect • pack • share · Punjabi voices with English subtitles

How to play

🌾 Harvest: swipe across or tap the golden wheat five times.

🐂 Load: drag or tap a wheat sack to put it in Baldev’s cart.

💛 Share: select a sack, then choose HOME, SEEDS or SHARING. You can also drag directly to a zone.

💬 Story: listen to the Punjabi voice and read captions below the scene. Use CC to show Punjabi and English, or hide captions. Use Continue or Enter to skip ahead.

🔈 Music and voice can be switched off independently. The mission never sends your voice or text to an API while playing.
